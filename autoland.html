3. Where does it break?

The same 50 flights, repeated with stronger and stronger gusts, to find the limit of the landing logic.

How the landing logic works

1. ApproachEvery 0.1 s the glider measures its ground speed and works out how fast it must sink to reach flare height just before the aim point. The aim point is placed before the target because the flare uses up distance.
2. FlareBelow 8 m the sink speed shrinks with height (height divided by 2), so the glider settles gently instead of dropping onto the ground.
3. Touchdown and scoreA flight passes if it lands within 30 m of the target and touches down slower than 0.5 m/s.

Limits: this is a simplified 2D simulation with perfect sensors, so results show the logic works under ideal measurements, not on a real glider. Height is stretched on screen so the descent is visible. Ground speed never drops below 3 m/s.
